## Concept
A swarm of living absolute-positioned DOM cards that morph their shapes in real time via CSS clip-path polygons generated by independent tiny local pure-JS MLPs. Causal input is solely DeviceOrientationEvent (beta/gamma tilt) plus a slow internal phase. Each MLP outputs continuous vertex offsets for a 6-vertex clip-path and velocity components. Agents compete by proximity: when two cards are close, the higher-fitness one seals and transfers a hashed fragment of its weights to the lower one with mutation. Best genome persists in IndexedDB and migrates across tabs via BroadcastChannel. Fully offline single-file prototype. No canvas continuum, no WebGPU, no particle lattice, no residual pointer history, no SVG paths.

This deliberately leaves the dominant recent continuum/particle WebGPU and residual/SVG-genome clusters. Core novel combination is hybrid DOM embodiment (physical cards as agents with evolving clip-path geometry) under pure orientation-causal local MLPs with proximity-gated sealed genome transfer. New building block for The Verse: morphing polygonal UI driven by device tilt and local evolving minds.

## Architecture
- Single index.html, self-contained, offline after load.
- 6 absolute-positioned div cards, each with independent 3-5-8 MLP (orientation features → 6 vertex dx/dy + vx/vy).
- Clip-path: polygon() updated each frame from MLP outputs + base shape.
- Fitness: inverse of path energy (smoothness of clip) + user click rewards + stability.
- On proximity < 60px: higher fitness seals and transfers 30% weights (mutated) to lower.
- IndexedDB key `verse-f696-clip`; BroadcastChannel `f696-genome`.
- Seal button persists best. Click card to reward. Drag to reposition temporarily.
- Fallback: synthetic slow oscillation + mouse velocity as tilt proxy if no DeviceOrientation (desktop).

## Interaction
- Request Orientation permission (mobile).
- Tilt device: cards morph and drift according to agent MLPs.
- Click cards to boost fitness and trigger potential genome transfer.
- Seal button to persist best genome.
- Reload or other tab receives via channel/IndexedDB.
- Drag cards to inject temporary position.

## Novelty
Leaves continuum WebGPU kernels, particle swarms, SDF, residual predictors, softbody, NCA, spectral, pointer-history, and prior orientation RNNs or SVG morphs. New: orientation-causal pure-DOM multi-agent genome competition controlling evolving CSS clip-path polygons.

## Failure modes
- DeviceOrientation requires secure context + permission; falls back to clock + mouse.
- IndexedDB may fail in private mode (session only).
- BroadcastChannel same-origin only.
- Tiny MLP yields emergent but limited morphs; clip-path may produce invalid polygons (clamped).
