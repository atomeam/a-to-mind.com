# F749 Chore rota generator
Plain name: Chore rota generator
Job: Enter the people in the household and the chores (how often each needs doing); the page builds a fair rotating schedule for the next several weeks, lets you adjust start day, then download the rota as CSV or an .ics calendar file, or copy a plain-text version. Everything stays on this device.
Input: List of people (names), list of chores with frequency (daily / every N days / weekly), optional start date
Keeps: CSV download, .ics calendar download, copyable text summary, plan saved in localStorage
Not on Verse yet: checked against existing tools (bill splitter, tip calculator, subscription tracker, debt snowball, teleprompter, packing list); no chore / task rota generator present

## Why this is a tool
People search for "chore chart generator", "fair chore schedule", "housework rota". They bring real names and real tasks and leave with a concrete schedule they can print, put on the fridge, or import into a calendar. No accounts, nothing leaves the browser.
