# F798 Spiking CSS3D Motion Mind

## Concept
A local Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) spiking neural network whose recurrent weights and membrane potentials form a sealed genome. Causal spikes are driven by DeviceMotion acceleration (x/y/z) and pointer motion. Each neuron is embodied as a living CSS 3D transformed DOM spark that jumps in 3D space on spike. User clicks provide reward that drives a simple STDP-like weight update. Multi-tab competition via BroadcastChannel adopts stronger genomes; persistence via IndexedDB. Pure physical UI embodiment — no canvas substrate, no WebGPU kernel.

## Novelty
Leaves the recent cluster of WebGPU CA/particle/LGA/DLA/attractor/Potts/RD substrates (F790–F797), living DOM rate-based RNN (F793), attention-field portals (F794), CSS3D battery RNN (F795), decision-tree kinematic chains (F796), and reaction-diffusion attention audio. Branches from prior orientation MLP/RNN, residual predictors, pure NCA, softbody DOM, spectral residuals, pointer-history fields, and k-means particles. Core novel combination is spiking (event-driven LIF) dynamics controlling pure CSS 3D DOM sparks under DeviceMotion causal input, with reward-gated STDP and cross-tab sealed genome competition. Not rate-coded networks, not simulation kernels, not canvas.

## Architecture
- N=48 LIF neurons, recurrent weights, membrane + recovery
- Sparks: absolute DOM divs with preserve-3d transforms
- Inputs: DeviceMotion accelerationIncludingGravity + pointer
- Reward: click → temporary weight potentiation on co-active pairs
- Seal: IndexedDB + BroadcastChannel mean-abs-weight competition
- Offline, progressive (pointer always works)

## Expected outcome
Open: floating sparks. Shake/tilt phone or move mouse → spikes cause spatial jumps and opacity flashes. Click sparks to strengthen connections. Seal persists; open second tab to compete genomes.

## Failure modes
- No DeviceMotion: pointer still drives residual input.
- IndexedDB blocked: session-only.
- High N on low-end: reduce N.

## Building blocks
Spiking embodiment of physical UI, DeviceMotion causal channel, STDP reward, sealed spiking genome competition. Expands non-rate local models and pure CSS3D minds for Verse.
