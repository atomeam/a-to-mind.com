# F748 Debt snowball planner
Plain name: Debt snowball planner
Job: Enter your debts with balances, interest rates and minimum payments plus any extra monthly amount you can put toward debt; choose snowball or avalanche order; get a month-by-month payoff schedule, total interest paid and payoff date, then download the schedule as CSV or copy the summary. Everything stays on this device.
Input: List of debts (name, balance, APR, min payment) and extra monthly payment amount; method toggle
Keeps: CSV download of the full schedule; copyable summary text; plan saved in localStorage for next visit
Not on Verse yet: checked against existing tools (bill splitter, tip calculator, subscription tracker, meeting cost calculator); no debt payoff planner present

## Why this is a tool
People search for "debt snowball calculator" or "avalanche vs snowball". They bring their real numbers and leave with a concrete schedule they can act on or share with a partner. No accounts, no data leaves the browser.

## Novelty relative to frontier cluster
Branches hard away from the dominant recent cluster of WebGPU particle/agent fields, orientation-driven RNNs/MLPs, mic-modulated forces, DOM portals, genome sealing, and swarm competition (F740–F747). This is a pure deterministic financial planning tool with local persistence only. No particles, no neural nets, no sensors, no spatial agents. The "local-AI" element is limited to the classic ordered prioritization algorithms themselves (lowest-balance-first vs highest-rate-first) plus optional simple what-if comparison. The capability added for Verse is a reusable offline schedule generator that other tools can later embed or link to.
