## Concept
A previously unused primary substrate for The Verse canvas: a WebGPU (or Canvas2D fallback) 2D incompressible fluid simulation (simple stable fluids / Navier-Stokes density + velocity) whose external forces are generated by a pure-JS multi-head attention mechanism over a sliding window of recent pointer positions, velocities, and residual prediction errors. Absolute living DOM portals sample the fluid density and velocity at their locations and pass those values as parameters to a CSS Houdini Paint Worklet that renders generative, fluid-influenced patterns directly on each portal. Clicking a portal delivers a local reward that performs a simple delta update on the attention projection weights. The entire mind (attention weights + optional fluid snapshot) seals to IndexedDB. Fully offline after load.

## Novelty
Leaves the dominant recent cluster of single-client sensor-driven local policies/attention/ES/RNN controlling living DOM portals, Houdini paints, WebGPU SDF/voxel sculptors, DEM granular, mic/speech synesthesia, and shared BroadcastChannel fields (F860–F872). Core novel combination is a continuum fluid field driven by local multi-head attention over pointer history, closed-loop with Houdini Paint Worklets that physically paint the portals from the sampled fluid state. Not pure density injection, not orientation MLP, not speech materialization, not shared multi-tab, not SDF sculpting.

## Architecture
- Single self-contained index.html
- Fluid: 64×64 density + velocity grids; simple Jacobi pressure, advection, force injection (WebGPU compute if available, else pure JS)
- Attention: sliding window of 16 pointer samples; 2 heads; pure JS QKV projections (small matrices); outputs force vector + magnitude
- Portals: 5 absolute-positioned DOM elements registered with a Paint Worklet that receives density, velocity magnitude, and hue bias
- Reward: click → positive residual reward → delta-rule on attention weights
- Seal: IndexedDB `verse-f873-attention`
- Fallbacks: no WebGPU → Canvas2D fluid; no Houdini → CSS gradients + transforms

## Mechanics
1. Pointer move records position/velocity into circular buffer.
2. Attention computes weighted forces and injects them into fluid.
3. Fluid advects and diffuses.
4. Each portal samples fluid, sets CSS custom properties, paint worklet redraws.
5. Click portal: reward, update attention weights, optional seal.
6. Reload: restore attention from IDB; fluid restarts from zero or sealed snapshot.

## Expected outcome
Move pointer: fluid swirls and portals paint living patterns that track local flow. Click portals to bias the attention mind toward preferred motions. Seal and reload: attention style persists. Interactive spatial local-AI whose continuum physics and generative paint co-evolve with the user’s pointer history.

## Failure modes
- No WebGPU: Canvas2D still fluid + paint.
- No Houdini support: portals fall back to CSS filters and gradients.
- IndexedDB blocked: session-only.
- High pointer rate: subsampled to 30 Hz.

## Building blocks
Reusable pure-JS multi-head attention force injector + WebGPU/Canvas fluid + Houdini Paint Worklet portal renderer. Enables future closed-loop continuum minds or worklet-driven physical UIs on Verse.
