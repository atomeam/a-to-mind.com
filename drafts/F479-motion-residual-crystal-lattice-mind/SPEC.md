# F479-motion-residual-crystal-lattice-mind

## Concept
Motion Residual Crystal Lattice Mind: Local DeviceMotion / DeviceOrientation events feed a Web Worker evolutionary linear residual predictor (velocity → next accel). The one-step residual vector anisotropically biases growth in a Canvas2D crystal cellular automaton (Moore neighborhood majority + residual-directed bias + stochastic nucleation). High-growth lattice sites attract or scale living DOM facet cards (position/hue/opacity driven by regional growth rate). Global CSS chrome morphs with residual magnitude and field entropy. State (CA grid snapshot + predictor weights + facets) seals to IndexedDB. Fully offline after load; device motion is the sole primary causal input (pointer click as secondary nucleation).

## Novelty
Leaves the dominant recent clusters of speech-vector recall (F478), mic-spectral policy (F476), WebGPU SDF/NCA/attention/Hebbian/particle residual, WebXR hand/gaze genome competition, DeviceOrientation MLP auctions, and pure lattice fields (F453–F477). Core novel combination is live device-motion residual as the exclusive anisotropic growth driver for a crystalline CA whose regional growth rates physically position and morph real DOM facet participants. Not audio spectral, not speech embeddings, not residual lattice injection into RD/NCA, not particle emission policies, not orientation wind on agents, not hypervector binding.

## Architecture
- Single index.html (progressive enhancement, offline-capable)
- Motion: DeviceMotionEvent / DeviceOrientationEvent (permission on iOS); fallback synthetic tilt
- Worker: population of 8 linear residual predictors (3→3); simple GA every ~1.5 s under residual-minimization fitness
- Crystal CA: 64×40 grid, Canvas2D; growth rule = majority + residual bias + nucleation
- Facets: 6 living DOM cards; positions CSS-transformed from regional samples; drag nucleates growth
- IndexedDB: seal/restore grid, weights, facet states
- CSS custom properties from residual variance / entropy for chrome morph
- No external dependencies

## Expected Outcome
Tilt or shake the device: residual spikes cause directed crystal growth; facets migrate to active growth fronts and change hue. Predictor improves at forecasting motion. Seal restores the living crystal field. Works offline; graceful without motion sensors (pointer nucleation).

## Failure Modes
No DeviceMotion (pointer fallback), iOS permission denied, IndexedDB private-mode, low-end throttles grid.

## Building Blocks
WebGPU compute crystal kernels, WebXR head-tracked residual, multi-session sealed crystal breeding, OPFS for full grid history.
