# F795 Seating chart maker
Plain name: Seating chart maker
Job: Paste a list of guests, choose how many tables, mark pairs who must sit together or apart; drag people onto tables to arrange them; download the chart as CSV or a printable PNG.
Input: Textarea for guest names (one per line), number of tables slider, two textareas or dynamic lists for keep-together and keep-apart pairs.
Keeps: Download seating as CSV; generate and download a visual PNG chart; last arrangement saved in localStorage for next visit.
